Privacy Policy

Earth Defender · Effective April 25, 2026 · Last updated April 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Earth Defender ("the App") handles information about you. The App is published by Ahmed Alemadi ("the Developer"). For questions, contact alemadi.us@gmail.com.

1. Summary

Earth Defender does not collect, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information. The App functions entirely on your device. No account is required, no tracking is performed, and nothing about your gameplay leaves your phone.

The only information stored is your local high score, which is saved in your device's local storage and remains on your device.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any contact information
  • Your location (precise or approximate)
  • Your contacts, photos, microphone, or camera
  • Device identifiers used for advertising
  • Any analytics or behavioral data
  • Any data through third-party SDKs

4. Children's Privacy

Earth Defender is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and you believe your child has provided personal information to us, please contact us at the email above.

5. Third Parties

The App currently does not integrate any third-party SDKs, advertising networks, analytics providers, or social platforms. Any future addition of in-app purchases or advertising will be disclosed through an updated version of this policy.

6. Apple App Store

The App is distributed exclusively through the Apple App Store. Apple has its own privacy practices described at apple.com/legal/privacy.

7. Your Rights

Because we do not collect personal data, there is no personal data to access, correct, delete, or port. For users in the European Union (GDPR) and California (CCPA): you do not have personal data with us to exercise rights against. If that changes in a future version, this policy will be updated.

8. Security

Because no data leaves your device, there is no transmission to secure. Local data is protected by your device's iOS sandboxing.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced through an in-App notice and/or App Store release notes before taking effect.
